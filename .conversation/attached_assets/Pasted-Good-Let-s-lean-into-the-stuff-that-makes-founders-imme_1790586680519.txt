# 14. The One I'd Build Tomorrow

### Pitch
"AI Business Rollup Machine"

This is the most dangerous one.

### Prompt
```text
Act as a business acquisition operator.

Identify small businesses that:

- Depend heavily on manual labor
- Use spreadsheets extensively
- Have retiring owners
- Generate recurring revenue
- Lack modern software

For each business:

1. Estimate automation potential
2. Estimate labor savings
3. Estimate acquisition value
4. Estimate post-acquisition profit growth

Build a rollup strategy showing how multiple businesses can be combined into a larger operation.
```

### Why investors go insane for this

Because it doesn't help someone start a business.

It helps someone **buy 10 businesses and automate them.**

That is where the next wave of AI money is likely headed: not content generation, but acquisition, optimization, consolidation, and automation of existing cash-flow businesses.

That's the category where SaaS goes from a $49/month tool to a company worth tens or hundreds of millions.
